⚠️ Reset — Sab Purana Data Delete Karein

Yeh sab OLD Indent/HOD/Store/CS/Management/PO records — Google Sheet aur is browser dono se — permanently delete kar dega, taaki aap bilkul fresh/live shuru kar sakein. Login (USER) aur Dropdown Settings touch nahi honge. Yeh undo nahi ho sakta.

🖊️ Existing Indent PDFs — Sab Signatures ke Saath Update Karein

Ab Indent PDF mein Indent Creator se lekar CS approval tak — jo jo approve kar chuka hai un sabka signature print hota hai. Jo Indents pehle se (is update se pehle) ban chuki hain, unki PDF purani hai — is button se un SAB ki Indent PDF ek saath dobara ban kar naye signatures ke saath Google Sheet par bhi update ho jayegi. Sirf wahi indents touch hongi jinme kam se kam ek signature (HOD/Store/Plant Head/COO/CS) pehle se maujood hai.

Add New User

Indent Creator ya Approval (HOD) role ke liye Department bhi assign karein — login karte hi unka Purchase Requisition form (ya HOD Approval queue) us Department par set ho jayega.

Role "Approval" (HOD) select ki hai — pehle Department select karein, phir yahan us Department ke HOD ka poora Naam bharein, taaki us HOD ka ID/Password sahi Department ke against bane.

Department yahan har role ke liye select ki ja sakti hai (na sirf Indent Creator) — ID/Password isi Department Code se banega. Indent Creator role ke liye Department set karne par unka Purchase Requisition form us par auto-select hoga AUR unko sirf apne hi Department ke Indent records dikhenge (baaki department ka data unhe nahi dikhega). "🔑 Auto" button dabane se password Department Code ke hisab se ban jaata hai (e.g. Mechanical department = MEC-XXX). Agar Department select nahi hai to pehle selected Role ke naam se banega.

🖊️ Signature Upload & Freeze (Admin Only)

Kisi bhi user ka signature image upload karke "Freeze" karein — us ke baad wo user jab bhi kisi bhi stage par sign karega, uska yeh image apne aap (auto mode) lag jayega, aur wo manually sign nahi kar payega. "Unfreeze" se wapas manual signing on ho jaati hai.
